The 5D+ Architecture

Each dimension adds a layer of territorial understanding—from spatial foundations to scenario space. This is SDT's architectural model, not a universal industry standard.

1D

Spatial Foundation

Coordinates, geometry, location, distances and territorial topology.

2D

Geospatial Intelligence

GIS layers, boundaries, networks, assets, routes and spatial relationships.

3D

Digital Representation

Terrain, buildings, infrastructure, BIM, volumetric scenes and digital twins.

4D

Temporal Intelligence

Time, events, state changes, history, dynamics and process sequences.

5D+

Territorial Intelligence & Scenario Space

Dependencies, rules, alternative states, consequence modeling, forecasting, optimization and decision support.

Main Principle

GIS shows where. BIM describes what is built. 3D shows how it is structured. 4D shows how it changes over time. SDT 5D+ explores what may happen when conditions change—and which decisions may lead to preferred outcomes.

In this framework, 5D+ means territorial intelligence and scenario space. In other industries, “5D” may refer to different concepts, including construction cost.
